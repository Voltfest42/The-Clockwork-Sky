For thousands of years the sky has run like clockwork: the stars wheel overhead, the Moon and planets keep their schedules, and on Christmas night 1758 a comet came back when gravity said it would. Part I takes that clockwork apart.

But the points of light on the dome are suns. They are born from collapsing gas, shine by fusion, and die as white dwarfs, neutron stars or black holes. Part II shows how we learned all that from starlight alone. Part III opens the machinery inside, down to the physics that decides each star's fate.

The course is being built one chapter at a time, starting with Part I. Chapters that aren't written yet are marked *coming soon*, and you can already see where each one fits.
